Everything You Want to Know About Botox in Longmont

Straightforward answers. No fluff.

What exactly is Botox and how does it work?
+
Botox is a purified protein derived from botulinum toxin. When injected in small, controlled amounts, it temporarily relaxes targeted facial muscles. This smooths out the lines those muscles create — like forehead wrinkles, frown lines, and crow's feet. The effect typically lasts 3 to 6 months. It doesn't affect your ability to make expressions when done correctly by a trained injector.
Does Botox hurt? What does the treatment feel like?
+
Most clients describe the sensation as a quick, light pinch — similar to a small bug bite. The needles used are very fine, and each injection takes only a second or two. The overall treatment is short. Most people are comfortably surprised by how manageable it is. Jessica uses a gentle technique that makes the experience far better than clients expect.
How long does Botox last in Longmont, CO?
+
Results typically last 3 to 6 months depending on the area treated, your metabolism, and how active the muscles are. Forehead and brow treatments may last closer to 4 months, while some areas last longer. Over time, with consistent treatments, many clients find they need less frequent touch-ups as the muscles become more relaxed long-term.
When will I see results after Botox injections?
+
You'll typically start noticing changes within 3 to 5 days of your treatment. Full results settle in around 10 to 14 days. This is also when Jessica recommends a quick follow-up check if needed. Don't judge your results in the first 48 hours — the product needs time to fully take effect in the muscle tissue.
Is Botox at Rewind Med Spa safe?
+
Yes. Botox has been FDA-approved for cosmetic use since 2002 and has an excellent long-term safety record. You're receiving medical-grade care in a licensed, professional setting. That distinction matters enormously.
What areas can Botox treat?
+
The most common treatment areas include forehead lines, the area between the brows (frown lines or "11s"), crow's feet around the eyes, and brow lifting. Botox can also be used for lip lines, neck bands, jaw slimming (masseter Botox), and hyperhidrosis (excessive sweating). Jessica will help you determine which areas make the most sense for your goals during your consultation.
What's the difference between Botox and fillers?
+
Botox relaxes muscles to smooth dynamic wrinkles — lines caused by movement. Fillers add volume to areas that have lost fullness over time — like lips, cheeks, and under-eye hollows. They work differently and address different concerns. Many clients benefit from both. During your consult, Jessica will explain which treatment (or combination) aligns with your specific goals.
Will Botox make me look frozen or unnatural?
+
Not with the right injector. The "frozen look" typically comes from over-treatment or poor technique. Jessica's philosophy is centered on natural results. She treats conservatively and strategically so that you still look like yourself — just refreshed. You'll still be able to smile, raise your brows, and show full expressions. Her clients consistently describe results as subtle and believable.

How do I prepare for a Botox appointment?
+
A few simple steps help optimize your results. Avoid blood thinners like aspirin, ibuprofen, fish oil, and vitamin E for about a week before treatment. Skip alcohol for 24 hours beforehand. Arrive with a clean face — no heavy makeup needed. Come hydrated. Jessica will go over everything with you during your consultation so you're fully prepared before your treatment day.
Is there downtime after Botox treatment?
+
Very little. Most clients return to their normal routine immediately after. You may notice minor redness or small dots at injection sites, but these typically resolve within 30 minutes to a couple of hours. For the rest of the day, avoid strenuous exercise, excessive heat (like saunas), lying flat for extended periods, and rubbing the treated areas. Beyond that, you're good to go.

What's the difference between Botox, Dysport, and Xeomin?
+
Botox, Dysport, and Xeomin are all botulinum toxin-based neuromodulators that work similarly to relax facial muscles and smooth wrinkles. The primary differences lie in their formulation, unit dosing, and how quickly they onset. Dysport tends to spread a bit more and may onset slightly faster, making it useful for certain larger areas. Xeomin is a "naked" neurotoxin with no accessory proteins, which some clients with Botox resistance prefer. Jessica can discuss which option might be best suited for your specific anatomy and treatment goals during your consultation.
How do I know if I need Botox or fillers — or both?
+
Great question — and it's one Jessica addresses in every free consultation. Generally: if your concern is lines caused by facial movement (forehead, frown lines, crow's feet), Botox is typically the right choice. If your concern is lost volume, sagging, or hollowing (cheeks, lips, under-eyes, nasolabial folds), fillers are usually more appropriate. Many clients benefit from both, and combining them strategically often produces the most natural and comprehensive results. There's no one-size-fits-all answer — which is exactly why that initial conversation matters so much.

If you're experiencing jaw pain from clenching, or bothersome sweating in the underarms or palms, ask Jessica during your consultation whether Botox might be appropriate for your situation.
What is masseter Botox and can I get it in Longmont?
+
Masseter Botox refers to injections placed into the masseter muscle — the large jaw muscle responsible for chewing and clenching. It's used for two primary purposes: jaw slimming (creating a more oval or refined facial silhouette) and relief from teeth grinding (bruxism) and TMJ-related jaw pain and tension. Results typically last 4 to 6 months. Yes, this treatment is available at Rewind Med Spa in Longmont. Discuss it with Jessica during your free consultation to determine if you're a good candidate.
